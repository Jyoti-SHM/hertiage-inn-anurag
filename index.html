Exquisite Dining at Hotel Heritage Varanasi

Embark on a sacred culinary journey at Hotel Heritage Varanasi, where pure vegetarian Banarasi traditions meet world-class dining in a regal setting. Indulge in authentic local flavors with our iconic Baati Chokha, tangy Tamatar Chaat, and a lavish Banarasi Royal Thali, along with timeless desserts like saffron Thandai and seasonal Malaiyo. Beyond Banaras, our 100% pure veg menu offers aromatic Awadhi biryanis, melt-in-your-mouth paneer specialties, artisanal Pan-Asian dim sums, and hand-crafted Continental classics—all prepared to perfection.
