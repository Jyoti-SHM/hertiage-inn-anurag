Pure Vegetarian • Est. Banarasi Kitchen

A Sacred Feast, Served with Devotion

Just steps from the ghats where the Ganga has carried prayers for five thousand years, our restaurant serves a 100% pure-vegetarian table built on Banarasi tradition. Every thali begins the way this city begins its mornings — unhurried, fragrant with ghee and temple incense, and generous with welcome. Our kitchen keeps faith with the old recipes of Kashi while setting an equally confident table for Awadhi, Pan-Asian and Continental palates.

"In Kashi, even a meal is an offering — we simply set the table where the Ganga can watch."

Signature Tastes of Kashi

  • Banarasi Royal Thali A full ceremonial spread of seasonal sabzis, dals, kachori-sabzi and hand-rolled rotis.
  • Baati Chokha Slow-baked wheat dumplings with smoky roasted-vegetable mash and pure ghee.
  • Tamatar Chaat Varanasi's own tangy street classic, plated for the dining table.
  • Saffron Thandai & Seasonal Malaiyo Cloud-light, saffron-laced desserts served only through the winter months.

Beyond Banaras

  • Awadhi Biryanis Dum-cooked, fragrant, finished the Lucknawi way.
  • Paneer Specialities Melt-in-the-mouth preparations from a dedicated paneer kitchen.
  • Pan-Asian Dim Sums Hand-folded, steamed to order.
  • Continental Classics Familiar comfort, plated with a Banarasi hand.
