Our Story

A Home for Travellers on the Ghats of Kashi

Hotel Heritage Inn was born from a simple belief — that every traveller arriving in Varanasi, the oldest living city on earth, deserves a warm, unhurried welcome. Tucked in Shivala, minutes from the ghats, we blend the quiet dignity of an old Banarasi household with the comforts modern guests expect. What began as a small family-run guesthouse has grown into a trusted address for pilgrims, explorers and business travellers alike, without ever losing the personal touch that started it all.

  • Family-run since day one
  • Steps from the River Ganges
  • Rooted in Banarasi hospitality
  • Guest care, around the clock

Our Journey

Milestones Along the Ganges

Y1
The Beginning
A Guesthouse Opens in Shivala

Hotel Heritage Inn opens its doors near the Anandmayi Hospital, offering a handful of simple, spotless rooms to pilgrims visiting the ghats.

Y2
Growing Roots
The Kitchen Finds Its Voice

Our pure-vegetarian Banarasi kitchen takes shape, serving Baati Chokha and Tamatar Chaat to guests who keep coming back for the flavours.

Y3
Expanding Comfort
New Rooms, Same Warmth

Deluxe, Executive, Family and Duplex rooms are added, each designed to suit a different kind of traveller — without changing how we treat them.

Today
Present Day
A Trusted Address in Varanasi

Today, Hotel Heritage Inn welcomes travellers from across the world, pairing modern amenities with the timeless hospitality Kashi is known for.
